summary: This course will develop students` knowledge of 
(1) geo-spatial measurements of  common civil, environmental and architectural engineering projects; 
(2) the types of  instruments, techniques and detailed 	procedures used for making geospatial measurements in a 	field environment; 
(3) the techniques involved in  collecting, processing and 	plotting the types of geospatial data necessary for  	constructing engineering-scale maps; 
(4) understanding of spatial reference frameworks; 
(5) effective approaches to setting up and presenting solutions 	of  positioning/location problems.
